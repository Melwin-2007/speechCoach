Good teamwork is not simply a group of people working on the same task. It requires communication, responsibility, and trust. Each person should understand their role while also understanding the larger goal of the team. When problems appear, blaming individuals rarely solves them. A better approach is to identify what happened, communicate clearly, and decide what should happen next. Different people also bring different strengths. One person may be good at explaining ideas, another at organizing information, and another at solving technical problems. Strong teams use these differences rather than expecting everyone to work in exactly the same way. When people communicate openly and take responsibility for their work, a team can achieve more than its individual members working separately.
