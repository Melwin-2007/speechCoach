Technology has changed the way we communicate, learn, and work. A message can travel across the world in seconds, and information that once required hours of research can now be found almost instantly. But technology is most useful when it supports human connection rather than replacing it. A video call can bring families together, an online classroom can connect students with teachers, and a simple message can remind someone that they are not alone. The challenge is to use technology with intention. We should choose tools that make our lives more productive and connected, while still making time for conversations, relationships, and experiences that happen away from a screen.
