Protecting the environment is not only the responsibility of governments or large organizations. Everyday decisions also influence the world around us. The way we travel, consume energy, use water, and manage waste can collectively make a significant difference. At the same time, individual action should be supported by better systems. Cities can improve public transportation, companies can reduce unnecessary waste, and communities can create cleaner public spaces. Environmental protection is therefore both a personal and a collective responsibility. We do not need every person to make a perfect choice every day. We need millions of people, organizations, and communities to make better choices consistently.
